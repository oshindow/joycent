Joycent: Multi-Accent TTS via Disentangled Accent Modeling and Layer-Specific Conditioning

Xintong Wang · Junchuan Zhao · Ye Wang

School of Computing, National University of Singapore, Singapore

↗ GitHub Code ◫ Paper

Joycent is a diffusion-based accent TTS framework that synthesizes speech in a target accent while preserving speaker identity — by separating accent and speaker information in both representation learning and generation conditioning. Using WhisAID to learn speaker-disentangled accent representations and layer-specific conditional layer normalization to inject accent and speaker information at different stages of the text encoder, Joycent enables robust synthesis for seen and unseen speakers, including challenging cross-accent settings where the speaker and accent prompts come from different accents.
